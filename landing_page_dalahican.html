A Coastal Paradise in Lucena

Dalahican is a vibrant barangay located in Lucena City, Quezon Province. Known for its stunning sunsets, fresh seafood, and warm hospitality, this coastal community represents the heart of Philippine fishing culture.

From the bustling fish market at dawn to the tranquil evenings by the shore, Dalahican offers an authentic glimpse into the lives of fisherfolk who have called these waters home for generations.

5K+ Residents
50+ Fishing Boats
100+ Years of History

Why Dalahican?

Experience the unique charm of coastal living with these community highlights

🐟

Fresh Seafood

Enjoy the freshest catch of the day straight from the fishing boats. From talakitok to sugpo, experience authentic coastal cuisine.

🌅

Sunset Views

Witness breathtaking sunsets over Tayabas Bay. The golden hour here paints the sky in spectacular hues of orange and purple.

⛵

Fishing Heritage

Learn about traditional fishing methods passed down through generations. Join boat tours and experience the fisherfolk way of life.

🤝

Strong Community

Be part of a tight-knit community that looks after one another. Experience genuine Filipino bayanihan spirit in action.

🏖️

Beach Access

Relax on the sandy shores and enjoy the calm waters. Perfect for family outings and weekend getaways from the city.

🎣

Island Hopping

Take boat trips to nearby islands and hidden coves. Discover pristine beaches just minutes away from the shore.
